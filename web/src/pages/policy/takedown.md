## Reporting

Use **Report a problem** at the top of any tool page and choose **Malware, abuse or a legal problem**, or run `ot report <namespace/name>`. You don't need an account, and the report goes only to the registry's reviewers. Reasons: malware or suspicious behaviour, a policy violation, impersonation, copyright or license problems, or something else.

A tool that simply doesn't work, shows an error or is out of date goes to the public **Issues** tab from the same
button, so others can confirm it and the maintainers can answer; without an account it is counted instead (see
[Report a problem](/docs/community#report-a-problem)). Posts in issues can be flagged to a moderator; personal data
in a post is hidden at once and removed by staff (see the [community guidelines](/policy/community)).

For copyright notices, legal requests, or anything you can't put in the form, write to [abuse@otinstaller.com](mailto:abuse@otinstaller.com). Security problems in the registry or `ot` go to [security@otinstaller.com](mailto:security@otinstaller.com).

## What happens next

- **First response** within 72 hours, and within 24 hours for malware or credible harm. With evidence of malware, any reviewer can remove a version at once; a second reviewer confirms afterwards.
- **Actions**, from least to most severe: dismiss, ask the author to fix, yank a version, remove a version or the package, suspend the namespace, ban the account.
- **Authors** are told what was done and why, and can appeal once by replying to that email.

## When a removed version is installed

Removed versions stop resolving at once. `ot` refuses to run them and tells anyone who has one installed how to remove it. Every removal appears on the [transparency page](/policy/transparency) with its reason category and date.
