The registry is for tools and guides that support lawful investigation: due diligence, threat intelligence, journalism, security research and similar work, using publicly available or properly licensed data.

## Not allowed

- Tools designed to track, monitor or harass a specific private individual, or stalkerware of any kind.
- Credential stuffing, password spraying, account takeover, or tools that use leaked credentials to log in.
- Tools whose main purpose is evading CAPTCHAs, bot detection or platform bans.
- Exploits or tools for unauthorized access to systems.
- Tools that bundle or redistribute leaked databases or illegally obtained personal data.
- Malware, cryptominers, telemetry the README does not disclose, or obfuscated code without a stated reason.
- Impersonation of other tools, vendors, agencies or people.

## Guides

- Examples use fictional data, the author's own data, or public figures in their public role. No guides built around a real private individual.
- No step-by-step instructions for anything this policy prohibits (stalking, account takeover, evading platform bans).
- Guides whose techniques touch personal data include a short scope and legal note.

## Honest descriptions

Each tool declares whether it contacts the target, logs in to accounts and collects personal data. Reviewers check these answers; wrong ones are a policy violation.

## Enforcement

Reviewers check every new publisher's first release. After that, reports from anyone can lead to a fix request, a yanked or removed version, a suspended namespace or a banned account. See [reports and takedowns](/policy/takedown).
