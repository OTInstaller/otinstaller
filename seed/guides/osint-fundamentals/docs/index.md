# OSINT fundamentals

Open-source intelligence (OSINT) is answering a question from information anyone can lawfully get to: public
websites, records, archives, images and the metadata they carry. The tools in the registry make the collecting fast.
What makes a finding hold up is the method around them, and that is what this guide is about. Read it before the
investigation playbooks; each of them assumes it.

**You will learn:**

1. [The method](01-method.md): scope, collect, pivot, verify, record and report.
2. [Opsec for the investigator](02-opsec.md): what your own requests reveal and how to keep your work separate.
3. [Legal and ethical basics](03-legal-and-ethics.md): purpose, personal data, and where to stop.
4. [Recording and reporting](04-record-and-report.md): a findings log someone else can check.

| A social media profile | [Social media profiles](https://otinstaller.com/guides/osintverse-community/social-media-profiles) |
