# Cryptocurrency address tracing playbook

Balance, history and counterparties of a Bitcoin or Ethereum address, one hop at a time.

Read it on the [OTInstaller Registry](https://otinstaller.com/guides/osintverse-community/crypto-address-tracing) or in your terminal:

```
ot guide read osintverse-community/crypto-address-tracing
```

The pages are in [`docs/`](docs/index.md). Corrections and additions are welcome as pull requests; releases are published with the **otinstaller release** workflow.
