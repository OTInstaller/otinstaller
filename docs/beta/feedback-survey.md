# End-of-beta survey (draft)

About five minutes. Send it on day 10 of the beta to everyone invited, including people who didn't start (their
answers to Q1–Q3 matter most). Any form tool works; keep the question order. Answers feed the launch go/no-go in
[../launch-checklist.md](../launch-checklist.md).

## About you

1. **What did you do during the beta?** (choose all that apply)
   - Installed `ot` · Ran tools · Followed a guide · Published a tool · Published a guide · Didn't get started
2. **If you didn't get started or stopped early, what got in the way?** (free text)
3. **Your operating system:** Windows · macOS (Apple silicon) · macOS (Intel) · Linux (which?) · other

## Installing and running (investigators)

4. **How easy was it to install `ot` and get your first result?** 1 (very hard) to 5 (very easy)
5. **How long did that take?** under 5 min · 5–15 min · 15–60 min · over an hour · didn't manage
6. **Which tools or guides did you use, and did they work?** (free text)
7. **Was anything about running tools unclear or surprising** (Docker, API keys, where results are saved, the
   trust prompts)? (free text)

## Publishing (authors)

8. **Where is your tool hosted?** GitHub · GitLab · both
9. **How long did it take from signing in to a published version?** under 15 min · 15–60 min · over an hour ·
   not published yet
10. **Which step was the hardest?** sign-in · import / drafting `otinstaller.yaml` · release workflow or pipeline ·
    scans and review · other (say which)
11. **Did anything make you consider giving up?** (free text)

## Overall

12. **How likely are you to recommend OTInstaller to a colleague?** 0–10
13. **What is the one thing we should fix or add before the public launch?** (free text)
14. **What would make you use it every week?** (free text)
15. **May we quote you (by name or anonymously) in the launch post?** yes, by name · yes, anonymously · no
16. **May we contact you about your answers?** yes (email) · no

---

### Reading the results

- Q1–Q3 and Q5: where people drop off. A "didn't manage" on Q5 for any OS is a launch blocker until fixed.
- Q9–Q11: authors. The plan's exit criterion is 10+ external authors published, at least 3 from GitLab. The admin
  console's Beta tab has the counts; the survey explains the gaps.
- Q12: NPS. With about 20 answers, read the comments rather than the score.
